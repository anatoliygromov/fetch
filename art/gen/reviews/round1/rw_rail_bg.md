7. View: PASS - Front-on, flat tubes and posts, foot plates on the ground line, no top or inside.
8. Fit: PASS - In the screen 1 and screen 4 frames at 1x and 3x it reads as a quay railing behind the tree trunk, the bench and the planter, and is clearly not the obstacle fence (28 px, baluster row, 1 px outline, near-black). It is the sparsest piece beside the lamp post and bench (no caps, no brackets), so it is the weakest on density, but not off the crispness bar.
9. Read: PASS - Reads at 1x as a plain tube railing; a child will not mistake it for the fence she climbs (50 px, three horizontal tubes, no balusters, faded lilac-grey). No text.

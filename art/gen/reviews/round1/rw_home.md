5. Shading: PASS - Per-material ramps of 3-4 (plaster pl/pl_sh/pl_dk, stone 4, door 3, glass 2), light from the upper left, every projecting part throws a 2-3 px shadow down and right, recesses shaded top and left; lone pixels 0.0 %, no blotches. Two stray pl_dk pixels (168,110,62) at the door knobs (image x 60 and 67, row 126) are plaster brown on a green leaf, harmless but wrong material.
6. Palette: FAIL - 17 of its 18 colours are not in art/palettes/riverwalk.gpl (only the brass 244,204,105 is; the same is true of the approved bench, bin and lamp, so the gpl looks stale) and none are shared with building, gable or cafe. The "white-painted" window frames and curtains are cream (250,240,220) and (228,212,182), no white. Darks are acceptable (ink 40,42,54). Needs a producer decision on which palette is binding before this can pass.
7. View: PASS - Front-on elevation; the string course, entablature and sills are thin bands with a lit top and a shaded edge; no tops, sides or insides are drawn.
8. Fit: PASS - At 1x and 3x beside bench and bin it is as crisp: flat fills, 0 % lone pixels, parts stamped on a grid, windows pixel-identical. It is calmer and less dense than the bench, which suits the back layer. The see-through quoin strip (item 4) is the only thing that breaks it.
9. Read: PASS - Door, fanlight, window, lantern and pipe read at 1x as a friendly Stockholm town house, no text. The tie-back curtains turn the glass into an egg or vase shape, which may read oddly to a child, but it still reads as a window.

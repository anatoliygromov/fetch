# Child playtest kit (River Walk)

One 20-minute session. The goal is to see the game through a 7 year old's eyes before the gold pass, so her stops, smiles and questions decide what we fix first.

## Before you start (5 minutes)

1. Download `FETCH.love` from the latest release: https://github.com/anatoliygromov/fetch/releases
2. Double-click it to open it in LÖVE, as with earlier builds. A gamepad works if one is connected; otherwise use the keyboard.
3. Choose START GAME. River Walk is level 1.
4. Have this sheet and a pen ready, or record voice notes on your phone.

## The one rule

**Don't help.** Don't explain controls or point at the screen. If she asks, say "what do you think?" Only step in if she has been stuck for 2 minutes or is upset. Then note what you said and when.

## Controls, if she asks after trying

Arrows or the d-pad move, A or Space jumps (Up also jumps, hold for a big jump), Down crawls, X picks up and gives the ball, Y or Z barks, B or Shift walks slowly.

## What to write down

| When (min:sec, or "near the boats") | What happened | What she did or said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | | |

Note especially:
- **Stops:** any moment she stands still for more than 10 seconds. Where was she, and what was she looking at?
- **Hits and lost lives:** what hit her, and did she see it coming?
- **Confusion:** "what do I do?", "where do I go?", pressing random buttons.
- **Delight:** laughs, "again!", "look!", talking to Pepper.
- **Reading:** does she read the hint panels? Can she?
- **The end:** does she understand she must give the ball to Mia?

## Five questions after playing

1. What was the best part?
2. What was hard or annoying?
3. What is the dog's name? What does she have to do?
4. Was anything scary or unfair?
5. Do you want to play again? Which part?

## Send it back

A photo of this sheet or the voice notes in the producer chat is enough. They become the gold-pass fix list.
